Fee Distribution & Revenue Model

Transparent smart-contract fee splits across tiers.

KOLPulse enforces a fixed total transaction fee of 2.0% across ecosystem activities. This fee is automatically distributed by the smart contracts between the Creator (Caller) and the Platform Treasury:

Standard Tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold)

  • • Caller Allocation: 1.0%
  • • Platform Treasury: 1.0%

Platinum VIP Tier (Override)

  • • Caller Allocation: 1.2% (Higher reward for top creators)
  • • Platform Treasury: 0.8%

Dashboard & Watchlist

Your personal command center and portfolio overview.

The Dashboard gives you an instant overview of your active P&L, paper balance or connected trading wallet, active snipes, and your favorite tokens.

  • • Watchlist: Click the star icon next to any token in the feeds to instantly add it to your personal tracking watchlist.
  • • Active Snipes & Sell Menu: Manage your open positions and instantly sell 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% via the dropdown menu.

Apex Quantum Sniper & Exit Shield

Zero-latency mempool execution and automated protection.

The Apex Sniper scans the mempool in real-time with an optimal RPC latency of ~4ms.

  • • Target Lock: Click on any token in the scanner to lock its Contract Address (CA) into the firing target view.
  • • Exit Shield (TP/SL): Set your desired Take Profit (%) and Stop Loss (%) and click Arm Take Profit / Stop Loss to automatically protect your position.
  • • Nuclear Dump: Instantly market-sell all your tokens in case of emergency.

Callers Hub & Treasury

For creators dropping alpha and claiming escrow rewards.

Content creators and alpha callers use the Callers Hub to publish calls and collect rewards.

  • • Drop Alpha: Input your CA, select your hype factor (e.g., 100x Gem or Degen Play), and choose between a Standard or Premium Snipe call.
  • • Treasury Claims: Your earned SOL from fee-splits flows directly into your escrow balance, ready to be claimed to your connected wallet with a single click.
